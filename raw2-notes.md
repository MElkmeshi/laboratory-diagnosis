## Term Definitions

### ② Thrombasthenia

Thrombasthenia is a hereditary qualitative platelet disorder (e.g. Glanzmann thrombasthenia), defect in GPIIb/IIIa characterized by defective platelet aggregation despite normal platelet numbers, resulting in prolonged bleeding time, petechiae, epistaxis, and impaired primary hemostasis.

### ⑤ Isosthenuria

Isosthenuria is the excretion of urine with a specific gravity similar to protein-free plasma (about 1.008–1.012), indicating inability of the kidneys to concentrate or dilute urine, commonly seen in renal failure.

### ① Fat Saponification

Fat Saponification is the process where fat binds to dietary Ca²⁺ in alkaline media of intestine forming soap.
- Result: Hypocalcemia ⇒ tetani
- Appear as white chalky precipitates.

## Thyroid Disorders

### Dogs vs Cats

| | Dogs | Cats |
|---|---|---|
| Condition | Hypothyroidism | Hyperthyroidism |
| Cause | Lymphatic thyroiditis (immune-mediated destruction) or idiopathic | Adenoma, sometimes idiopathic |
| BMR | ↓ BMR (systemically) | ↑ BMR |

### Screening Tests

- Total T4 — Thyroxine + Albumin/globulins (mainly)
- Free T4 (more active) = prothyroid hormone
- T4 → T3 (active) by deiodinase
- TT3 (total T3)
- Free T3
- TSH [formed in hypothalamus, stored in Pituitary]

### Diagnosis

- ↓ TT4 = Hypothyroidism
  - In early stages TT4 borderline → measure TSH ↑
- **Hypothyroidism = ↑ TSH + ↓ TT4**
- ↑ Free T4 = Hyperthyroidism
  - TT4 borderline → measure Free T4 ↑

### Clinical Signs of Hypothyroidism

- Weight gain
- Intolerance to cold
- Tachycardia
- Bilateral alopecia

### Goiter

Goiter occurs in both hypo/hyperthyroidism — atrophied thyroid cells.

[Hypo] ↑↑↑ TSH → ↑ stimulation → Goiter

### Myxedema (severe, untreated Hypothyroidism)

**Causes:**
- ↓ Thyroid peroxidase
- ↓ Deiodinase enzyme
- ↓ CO
- Hypotension → organ failure
- Bradycardia
- Coma - death
- Hypothermia
- ↑ Cold intolerance
- Weight gain
- Non-regenerative anemia (macrocytic or microcytic)

**Pathophysiology:** Mucopolysaccharides not breaking to monosaccharides → accumulate under skin or in organs → non-pitting edema (puffy face, exudate edema).

### Cat Hyperthyroidism

- Polycythemia
- Loss of weight despite good appetite
- Heat intolerance
- Tachycardia
- Cardiac hypertrophy
- ↑ CO

### Treatment

- **Cause of death in cats = cardiac arrest**
- **Dog:** Levothyroxine 0.5 → 0.1 mg/kg, 2–3 times/day, orally
- **Cat:** Metimazole or capromazole (life-long) 0.2 → 1.5 mg / 5–10 mg
  - Or radioactive iodine IV I¹³¹
  - → Sometimes causes hypothyroidism → levothyroxine

### Euthyroid Sick Syndrome (ESS) / Non-Thyroidal Illness

Reversible T3 ↓ in chronic diseases due to prevention of T4 → T3 conversion.

## Exocrine Pancreatic Insufficiency (EPI)

(14) Exocrine pancreatic insufficiency (acute → chronic) — 90% damage

- Saline dilution test: clot → agglutination / normal → Rouleaux formation

**EPI Signs in Dogs:**
- Polyphagia (due to malabsorption)
- Weight loss
- Steatorrhea (fats in feces)
- Crietorrhea (undigested fibers in feces)
- ↓ Elastase

**Dog case:** Chronic weight loss despite good appetite, ↑ Steatorrhea, ↓ Serum trypsin-like → EPI

## Renal Tubular Acidosis

- **Type I** — Defect in excretion of Hydrogen ions; defect in DCT
- **Type II** — Defect in reabsorption of bicarbonate; defect in PCT (Fanconi Syndrome)
- **Type IV** — Low renin

## Key Facts

- **Azotemia** = ↑ Creatinine + ↑ BUN
- **Chronic Kidney Disease signs:** Polyuria, polydipsia, azotemia, isosthenuria
- **Hepatocellular injury ratio:** 2:17:13
- **75% damaged** (kidney threshold for signs)
- Fanconi anemia [defect in DNA repairing ⇒ Aplastic anemia]
- Fanconi Syndrome [defect in reabsorption of nutrients in PCT of kidney]

## Hemolytic Disorders

### Hepatic Carcinoma

- Sudden increase
- ↑ Alkaline phosphatase
- ↑ Gamma glutamyl transferase

### Pappenheimer Bodies

- Abnormal iron-content, cluster-like
- Iron → Prussian blue stain

### Hemolytic Uremic Syndrome (HUS) / HELLP

- HELLP = Hemolysis, Elevation of Liver enzymes, Low Platelets
- Fragmented RBCs: Schistocytes
- E. coli (Shiga toxin)
- Thrombocytopenia (HUS)
- Bloody diarrhea
- Bleeding tendency
- Renal failure

**Atypical HUS:**
- Activation of complement system
- No bloody diarrhea
- Severe kidney injury
- Schistocytes

## Miscellaneous Conditions

### Acetaminophen Toxicity in Cats

Highly fatal even in low doses due to lack of specific liver enzyme glucuronosyl transferase needed to break the drug safely.

### Infectious Mononucleosis ("Kissing Disease")

- Caused by Epstein-Barr Virus (EBV)
- Signs: Fever, sore throat (laryngitis), lymphadenopathy especially in neck and armpit, may be enlarged spleen/liver
- 2 weeks incubation period
- Treatment: rest / drinking water
- Especially in teenagers and young people
- Contagious disease

### Antiphospholipid Antibody Syndrome

Classical paradox (in vivo):
- Deep vein thrombosis (DVT)
- Pulmonary embolism
- Stroke
- Liver dysfunction / kidney failure [multiple organ dysfunction syndrome] (shrinkage)

### Photosensitization

- **Cattle:** Secretory photosensitization — Chlorophyll → Pheophorberin (conjugate with glutathione, oxidised)
- **Horse:** Pyrrolizidine alkaloids in Ivy wort (Poison Ivy plant) → liver cirrhosis → (photodynamic) → dermatitis
